    ABOUT

    I’m Yihan Zeng, an industrial design student working across physical installations, speculative interfaces and product systems.

    I study at Beijing University of Chemical Technology and am currently on exchange at the University of Genoa (DAD), Italy. I’m interested in what a designed object asks people to do — and who gets to make the decision.
