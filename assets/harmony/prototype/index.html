Lunch · 12:30 · the wall is clear

A small osteria in the hills behind Genoa. The menu follows the wild.

Every dish is one wild and one farmed ingredient, served side by side. How much of the plate is wild depends on how many of that species are out there today. The portion and the price stay the same.

The glass wall keeps time with lunch. Each plate that leaves the kitchen moves the lower row one pane; darker means too few, white means too many.

Today's menu

The wild today

Each morning the camera traps, seed nets and catch records are counted. Every species gets one number, from far too few to far too many. That number sets the share of wild on its plate, and every plate served carries it onto one pane of the glass: darker when there are too few, white when there are too many, clear when it is in balance.
