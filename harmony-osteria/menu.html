Four courses, one choice in each.

Every dish pairs one wild and one farmed ingredient. The wild share follows how many of that species are out there today, so it changes from day to day. The portion and the price do not.

Good to know
  • When a species is too scarce to take, its dish is not available that day.
  • The share in each dish is set at 07:30 from the latest counts and holds for the day.
  • Vegetarian menu on request when you book. We cannot cook without the listed allergens in every dish.
  • Wine by the glass from Liguria and Piemonte. Water is free.
