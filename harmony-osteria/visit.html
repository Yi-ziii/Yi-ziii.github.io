Questions
Can I choose more or less wild?
No. The share is set each morning from the counts and is the same for every table that day.
Allergies and diets
Tell us when you book. A vegetarian menu is available on request.
Children
Welcome at lunch. We have high chairs and a half portion of each course.
Cancelling
Free up to 24 hours before. After that, write to us and we will try to fill the table.
Large groups
For nine or more, write to booking@harmonyosteria.it.
Accessibility
One level from the car park to the table, with an accessible washroom.
